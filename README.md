> An immersive digital creative production studio showcase featuring fluid WebGL liquid distortion shaders, spatial ambient audio, interactive mouse trails, and cinematic camera transitions.

🔗 **Live Production**: [https://ergos-beryl.vercel.app](https://ergos-beryl.vercel.app)

---

## 🌊 Technical Highlights

- **Liquid Displacement Shaders**: GPU-driven fluid distortion reacting dynamically to mouse velocity.
- **Multi-Track Spatial Audio**: Procedural background ambience (`ambient.mp3`), transition stingers (`cameraTransition.mp3`), and tactile hover sound effects.
- **Cinematic Framing**: Dynamic viewport scaling and perspective warping.
- **Custom Matter CSS**: Bespoke physics-inspired design system.
